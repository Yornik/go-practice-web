The rules

Rule 1 is about practice, not a verdict on AI tools at work. At your job, use the tools your team trusts.

  1. 1

    Rule 1. Do not use an LLM. That includes chat assistants and AI autocomplete. In the Spire, this rule is etched in the lobby glass.

  2. 2

    Rule 2. Use documentation freely: go doc, pkg.go.dev, the Go spec, man pages and RFCs. Finding the answer in the docs is part of the skill.

  3. 3

    Rule 3. Try for at least 25 minutes before you reveal hint 1. Wait at least 10 minutes between hints.

  4. 4

    Rule 4. If you read the reference solution, close it, wait, and write your own version from memory.

  5. 5

    Rule 5. Read the explanation and the walkthrough after you pass, too. They show the trade-offs.
